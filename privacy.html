Privacy Policy

The short version

The App has no servers, no account system, and no analytics. Every sign you make stays on your device — we collect nothing, and we have nowhere to send it.

01

Information We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data. Specifically:

  • No servers, no account system: the App operates entirely on your device.
  • No analytics and no advertising: the App contains no analytics SDKs and no ads, and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites (for App Tracking Transparency purposes, we do not track you at all).
  • No payment data: purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your credit card number or bank information.
  • Support communications: if you contact us by email, we receive your email address and anything you send us, and use it only to respond to you.
02

Where Your Data Lives

Everything the App stores stays on your device:

  • The signs you create, your history, favourites, templates, and settings are saved in the App’s local storage on this device.
  • The StandBy bedside widget reads the sign you saved from a shared on-device container that only the App and its widget extension can access.
  • Deleting the App removes all of it. There is no cloud copy, because there is no cloud.
03

What Leaves Your Device

Nothing you create leaves your device unless you send it yourself:

  • The App’s only network communication is with Apple’s App Store, to process purchases and keep them up to date.
  • When you export a sign as a GIF or video, the file is handed to the system share sheet. Where it goes next — a message, a social app, your photo library — is your choice, made through Apple’s own sharing interface. We never see it.
  • StandBy is rendered by iOS itself, on your device.
04

Third-Party Services

  • Apple App Store: processes payments and refunds. Apple’s own privacy policy applies to any payment data Apple collects.

We do not use Firebase, Unity Analytics, AdMob, AppLovin, or any other analytics, advertising, or tracking service. The App contains no embedded web browser and loads no third-party web content.

05

Permissions the App Requests

Each permission is used only for the feature named, and the related data stays on your device:

Photos (add-only)
To save a GIF or video you exported to your photo library. The App can only add files; it cannot read, change, or delete your existing photos.

That is the whole list — the App does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, or notification permissions.

06

Your Controls

From the App you can, at any time:

  • Delete individual history entries, or clear your entire history in Settings;
  • Remove a saved bedside display; or
  • Uninstall the App, which removes all locally stored data.
07

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or a higher age where required by local law). Because we collect no personal data at all, we hold none from any age group.

08

Data Retention and Security

All data created by the App is retained only on your device, until you delete it or uninstall the App. We take reasonable technical measures appropriate to an on-device application. Because we operate no servers and collect nothing, there is no central store of user data that could be breached.

09

Your Rights

Whatever your jurisdiction — the GDPR in Europe, the UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA in California, the LGPD in Brazil, or your local equivalent — the practical answer is the same: LED Banner holds no personal data about you on any server we operate, so there is nothing for us to retrieve, export, or delete on your behalf. Data on your device is under your control (see Section 6).

If you have a legal right to a written confirmation of this, write to the address below and you will receive a written reply. If you have ever emailed us, we can delete that correspondence on request.

10

Refunds

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are billed by Apple. Refunds are handled by Apple under the App Store’s refund policy, not by us, because Apple processes the payment. We have no servers, receive no purchase data, and play no part in refund decisions.

11

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update this page and change the “Effective Date” at the top. If a change materially affects your rights, we will say so plainly in the App before it takes effect.
